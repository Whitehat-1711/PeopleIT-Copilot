# Company Code of Conduct & Ethics Policy
Owner: Legal & Compliance (compliance@company.com)
Last-Updated: 2026-01-10

## Workplace Professionalism and Harassment Prevention
The company enforces zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation based on gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age. All employees must complete mandatory annual anti-harassment training (POSH). Violations are investigated by the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) and can lead to immediate termination without notice.

## Conflict of Interest and Secondary Employment
Employees must avoid any personal, financial, or commercial interest that conflicts with their duty to the company. Dual employment ("moonlighting"), commercial freelancing, or serving as a director or consultant for any entity competing with the company is strictly prohibited without prior written sanction from the Chief Legal Officer.

## Gifts, Anti-Bribery, and Ethical Standards
Employees must comply with all applicable anti-corruption and anti-bribery statutes. Giving or accepting gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued in excess of ₹2,500 from vendors, clients, or business partners is prohibited. Any customary festive gifts received must be declared on the compliance portal.

## Social Media and Public Communication
Only designated corporate spokespersons are authorized to make public statements or speak to the press on behalf of the company. When posting on personal social media channels, employees must clarify that opinions are their own and must never disclose non-public company financials, roadmap details, client names, or proprietary code.

## Whistleblower Protection and Reporting Channels
Employees who observe or suspect illegal, unethical, or fraudulent behavior may submit a report anonymously via the independent Whistleblower Hotline or email `ethics-hotline@company.com`. The company guarantees complete confidentiality and strictly prohibits any retaliation against individuals raising concerns in good faith.
