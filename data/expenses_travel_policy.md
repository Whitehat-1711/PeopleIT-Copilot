# Company Expenses & Travel Policy
Owner: Finance Operations (finance@company.com)
Last-Updated: 2026-02-10

## Domestic Travel Guidelines
Domestic business travel must be booked at least 7 business days in advance via the corporate travel desk. Flight travel is economy class for trips under 6 hours. Hotel accommodations are capped at ₹6,500 per night in Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR) and ₹4,500 per night in other cities. Ride-hailing fares (Ola, Uber) for official transit are reimbursable against digital tax invoices.

## International Travel and Per Diem
All international business travel requires pre-approval from the Department Head and CFO at least 3 weeks prior to travel dates. Employees traveling internationally receive a daily per diem allowance of $75 USD for meals and incidentals. Overseas health and travel insurance is provided and mandated by corporate policy for the complete duration of the travel.

## Meal and Client Entertainment Expenses
Employees traveling on official business may claim up to ₹1,500 per day for personal meals when meals are not provided by the conference or host. Client entertainment meals require pre-approval from the Sales or Practice Director for any spend exceeding ₹5,000 per event. Itemized receipts detailing attendees and business purpose must accompany every entertainment claim.

## Expense Claim Submission and Reimbursement SLAs
All business expenses must be submitted through the Finance portal with itemized GST receipts within 15 calendar days of incurring the expense. Approved expense reports are processed and disbursed via direct payroll deposit within two weekly payment cycles (typically 10 business days from final approval).

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse personal grooming, hotel minibar charges, traffic fines or parking violations, airline seat upgrade fees, personal entertainment or movies, alcoholic beverages (unless formally approved as part of an official company banquet), or expenses for accompanying family members.
