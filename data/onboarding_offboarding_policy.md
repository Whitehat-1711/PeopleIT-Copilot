# Company Onboarding & Offboarding Policy
Owner: Talent & Operations (talent-ops@company.com)
Last-Updated: 2026-01-20

## New Hire Onboarding Process
New employees undergo a 3-day orientation program covering company culture, core values, security training, and HR compliance. Government IDs (PAN, Aadhaar, previous employment records) must be uploaded to the onboarding portal at least 5 business days prior to Day 1. Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy for their initial 60 days.

## Hardware and Account Provisioning Timeline
IT provisions corporate email, Slack, and single sign-on credentials 48 hours prior to the employee's start date. Company-issued laptops are dispatched via insured courier or handed over during Day 1 in-office induction. Standard software tool access requests must be initiated by the reporting manager during the pre-boarding phase.

## Probation Period and Confirmation
All regular full-time appointments are subject to an initial probation period of 6 months. A formal performance review is conducted at the 5-month mark by the reporting manager and department head. Upon successful review, an official written confirmation letter is issued by People Operations. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months if performance criteria are partially met.

## Employee Resignation and Exit Formalities
Employees submitting resignation must formally do so via the HR portal. The standard contractual notice period is 60 calendar days (30 days during probation). Employees must complete handovers and schedule an exit interview with their HR Business Partner during the final two weeks of employment.

## Asset Return and Full & Final Settlement
All company-owned property—including laptops, access badges, monitors, and corporate credit cards—must be returned to the IT desk on or before the last working day. The Full & Final (F&F) settlement statement and gratuity/leave encashment payout are finalized and transferred to the employee's bank account within 30 calendar days following asset clearance.
